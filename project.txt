Write an app to track people entering and exiting the building.

Business scenario:
We here at Company Inc. value security above everything else. We need to be sure that the people that enter our building, also exit it in the expected manner.
Employees, visitors and maintenance all get the same treatment: you come in, you must go out.

QA
Q: Who exactly enters your office?
A: The people that can enter our office are:
- Employees
- Visitors, which often are friends or family of our employees
- Maintenance, which are the people cleaning the office during afterhours

Q: Could you describe how you would want the tracking to work like?
A: We expect it to work as follows:
- A person enters the building through the main door
- The face of the person is registered in our database
- If the person enters our office for the first time, we make them fill out an extensive form to ensure they are safe to enter
- After that we also ask for the reason of the visit, duration, and who are they visiting
- Once all of that is filled and validated, we let the person go on their way, while keeping a close eye on them
- If ANY of the processes above trigger an alert we send in the security guards to escort the person outside

Q: What could trigger an alert?
A: The following can trigger an alert:
- The reason of visiting is malicious
- They are trying to visit a non-employee or an employee that is currently not at the office
- Their scheduled time has ended and they haven't exited the office
- Their scheduled time is longer than the employee's time in the company
- Anything that we consider suspicious

Q: Can we know more details about the employees' schedule?
A: They work 3 days a week from 8 AM to 8 PM. At any given time, at least 10 employees are in the building.

Q: Do the maintenance people come after 8 PM?
A: They come in at 9 PM and have untill 4 AM to clean the entire office. They are expected to enter and exit through the backdoor.

Q: What if an alert is triggered?
A: We want to be notified immediatly via any sort of communication method as well as logging the alert for further reference.
